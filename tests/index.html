Aug 18 Relaunch

181 pages the relaunch put live on August 18, 2026, outside the uploader queue. They are reported separately on purpose. They were not built by this program, so counting them as New Builds would overstate it, and 128 of them are pre-existing URLs whose WordPress post was recreated rather than rewritten, so a refresh lift number would not mean anything. They are also held out of the untouched-control pool used above.

WordPress records these posts as created Aug 6, but that is when the posts were made, not when the pages shipped. The 53 new URLs have no Search Console impressions at all before Aug 18, and almost all of them record their first impression on Aug 18 or the day after, so Aug 18 is the date every window below is anchored to. Note this is also the day Google began the August 2026 spam update, so the relaunch effect and the update cannot be cleanly separated in these numbers.

These pages are not a separate population from the cohort above. 29 of the 181 are also in the upload cohort, worth 2.4K clicks in the last 28 days. Those clicks are counted in both sections, so the two totals cannot be added. Rows carrying them are tagged also above in the table.
