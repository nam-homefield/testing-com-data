Numbers come from Stripe (paid invoices and refunds), Junction and Thimble (product names), and Google Analytics (organic visits). Prepared Thu, Oct 1, 2026, 3:35 PM ET (12:35 PM PT)

How this is counted
  • A paid order is a Stripe invoice that was paid. Retries by the same person for the same amount within 15 minutes count as one order.
  • Orders from staff, vendor and test addresses are removed before anything is counted.
  • Days are Pacific days. Weeks run Tuesday to Monday from launch on Tuesday, Aug 18, 2026.
  • Products are grouped by their name, using fixed word lists. A product whose name matches no list is shown as not yet categorized, and the report does not publish if there are too many.
  • Organic visits are engaged Google visits to www.testing.com from the United States, with our own browsing removed. A visit figure can move a little between versions while Google Analytics finishes counting.
  • Refunds count only refunds to customers on real orders.
