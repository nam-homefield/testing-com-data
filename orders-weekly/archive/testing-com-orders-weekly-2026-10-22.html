Orders and Revenue

  • 805 paid orders, $120,031 in revenue in the first 65 days.
  • 12.4 orders per day and $1,847 per day over the 65 full days (Aug 18 to Oct 21).
  • Average order value is $149.
  • STD testing is 71% of orders and 82% of revenue.
  • The best full week so far is Week 8, with 116 orders and $14,643.
  • Oct 20 was the busiest day so far with 30 orders. It was also the largest revenue day at $4,752.
  • Refunds total $5,402 across 59 refunds, so net revenue is $114,629.

Full STD Panels bring in the most revenue per order of any category: 21% of orders but 38% of revenue, about $278 per order. Prices for both STD panels were lowered on Sep 2 to be more competitive. In the 15 days before the price cut (Aug 18 to Sep 1), 16 Full STD Panels sold, about 1.1 a day, at an average of $307. In the 50 days since (Sep 2 to Oct 21), 150 sold, about 3.0 a day, at an average of $275. The Standard STD Panel went from 25 sold (about 1.7 a day) at an average of $157 to 156 (about 3.1 a day) at $151.
