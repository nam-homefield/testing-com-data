STD Testing GEOs fell from 1.53% to 1.25%, because Google Analytics recorded more of our sales last week (88%, against 71% before), so each recorded purchase stands for fewer orders. These are estimates. Last week Google Analytics recorded 104 purchase sessions for the 118 paid orders, 88% of them. Each page type's orders are its share of those 104 sessions, applied to the 118 paid orders.

The page types add up to the Total row. With the Direct or outside the United States line, they add up to the 118 paid orders last week. That line is an estimate with no rate, because its visits are not counted.

Panel buyers often start on a city page, so the GEOs row gets that credit.

Previous 4 weeks CVR is estimated the same way, from the purchase sessions and paid orders of those four weeks.

A cell marked small sample size rests on fewer than 10 Google Analytics purchase sessions, so one purchase moves its rate a lot.

Every row counts engaged visits from the United States that are not Direct, with our own browsing removed, and the paid orders that match them, by the page the visit landed on. Monetized /tests/ pages come from the crawl of Nov 11, 2026.

Homepage is the page / and no other. Checkout pages are visits that landed on a page whose address starts with /checkout.
