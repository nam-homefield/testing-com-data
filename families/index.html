How this is built. Google Analytics cannot count orders on this site: purchase events report no money and often do not fire at all. Money comes from Stripe. Every checkout sits at a web address containing the order code, and Stripe stores the same code, so an order can be matched to the visit that reached that checkout, and from there to the page the visitor first landed on.

What it cannot tell you. Credit goes to the landing page of the same visit that reached checkout. Someone who found a page last week and came back later to buy is not counted here, so these are floors, not ceilings. Page types come from page_families.csv, rebuilt by reading the live site.
