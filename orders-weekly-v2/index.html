Orders and Revenue

  • 459 paid orders, $71,465 in revenue in the first 44 days.
  • 10.4 orders per day and $1,624 per day over the 44 full days (Aug 18 to Sep 30).
  • Average order value is $156.
  • STD testing is 72% of orders and 83% of revenue.
  • Weeks 4, 5, and 6 are the best full weeks so far, at 82 orders each.

Every STD test or panel order is counted under STD Testing, on the assumption that STD buyers start on an STD testing page.

Of the 156 STD orders in these four weeks that could be traced to a page, 137 (88%) started on an STD testing page.

These rates are estimates. Each is the paid orders in that row divided by the engaged visits to that row's pages. Not traced counts the other paid orders that could not be tied to a landing page, including those that arrived directly or from outside the US, and has no rate. Total is every paid order over all engaged visits.

A cell marked few orders rests on fewer than 10 orders, so one order moves its rate a lot.

Weeks run Tuesday to Monday on Eastern time for both visits and orders. An order paid late on a Monday night Pacific falls in the next week here, so these counts can differ from Week by Week. That moved 2 of these paid orders.

Paid time was missing on 320 of these paid orders, so their created time stands in.

Visits are engaged visits from the United States that are not Direct, with our own browsing removed, counted by the page they landed on. An order is credited to the page of the visit that first reached its checkout page before it was paid. Monetized /tests/ pages come from the latest page crawl on or before each week ends: Sep 11, Sep 14, and Sep 27.

Week 3 ends before the first page crawl, so it uses the crawl of Sep 11.
